# Founding institution selection

Select 3–5 institutions only after completing the legal-readiness gate. Score with primary evidence; do not score reputation by intuition.

## Hard gates

- Named authorised signatory and rights-clearance office.
- Current IP policy supplied and usable for student/faculty work.
- Agreement to the disclosure-state workflow and a target response time.
- Ability to contract and receive milestone payments.
- Consent to append-only provenance and negative-result records.
- At least five sourceable assets in one initial lane.

Failure on a hard gate means `not ready`, regardless of score.

## Weighted scorecard

Score each factor 0–5 and multiply by weight. Maximum weighted result is 500; normalise to 100.

| Factor | Weight | Evidence required |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Rights/disclosure operating capacity | 20 | Named office, policy, recent decision examples, SLA owner |
| Relevant asset pipeline | 20 | 5+ candidate records with evidence and contributor contacts |
| Contract/payment readiness | 15 | Signatory, contracting route, bank/tax documentation |
| Faculty and student participation | 10 | Named cohort lead and contributor onboarding commitment |
| Validation facilities | 10 | Facilities mapped to the initial lane, with pricing route |
| Data/security readiness | 10 | NDA, controlled access, retention and incident contacts |
| Industry proximity | 10 | Existing partners and travel/field-access practicality |
| Measurement commitment | 5 | Agreement to record hours, conversion, failures and cycle time |
