## No-fabrication rule

The alpha contains demonstration records. Candidate assets are publicly sourced, but public release is not the same as institutional authority to transact. No record may be labelled `institution-cleared`, no conversation may be counted, and no legal document may be labelled `approved` without the corresponding external evidence.
